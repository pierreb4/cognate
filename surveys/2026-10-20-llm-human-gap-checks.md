# Verification checks for the LLM-human gap survey (2026-10-20)

Each claim the survey note leans on was re-opened on its primary source on 2026-10-20, and the
number, date and attribution were compared with the slice draft that carried it. Verdicts:
**confirmed**, **corrected to X**, **not found** (the primary does not carry it), or
**partly confirmed** (the gist is confirmed, a detail is not).

The survey note is [2026-10-20-llm-human-gap.md](2026-10-20-llm-human-gap.md). The "Draft"
column names the reading pass's working draft and item that carried the claim (priors,
modeling, exploration, training, continual, closing); those drafts were merged into the note
and are not kept here.

The ARC-AGI-3 GPT-6 Astra figures (https://arcprize.org/blog/astra, 2026-09-03) and the GPT-5.5
0.43% (https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-gpt-5-5-opus-4-7-analysis, 2026-05-01) were verified
before this pass and were not re-fetched.

## What changed in the note because of these checks

- METR doubling is stated from METR's own post: 196 d long-run, 131 d post-2023, 89 d post-2024
  (TH1.1). The "105-day" and "3.5-month" figures are dropped.
- The Mythos horizon is stated as ">=16 h, at the suite's stated ceiling", not as 16.0 h or 17.4 h.
- The ConceptARC human figure is ~7% (v5), not ~8%.
- ZendoWorld's best agent is 45.5% (Gemini 3.1 Pro preview).
- GPT-6 Astra's ARC-AGI-2 95.0% is cited to ARC Prize's results page, not to Epoch's copy.
- Argon's ARC-AGI-3 rows are labelled Public Demo by ARC Prize and stay out of the headline.
- The ARC-AGI-3 human panel is 458 people, and its baseline definition changed (second-best ->
  median player), which the trust section reports.
- IntPhys 2's held-out human figure (92.44) moves from unverified to verified.
